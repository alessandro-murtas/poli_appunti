Let $A in CC^(n times n)$. The **spectrum** of $A$, denoted by $sigma(A)$, is the set of all its [[Eigenvalues and eigenvectors|eigenvalues]]:
$$
sigma(A) = \{lambda in CC: exists underline(v) in CC^n without {underline(0)}space.en  text(s.t.) space.en  A underline(v) = lambda underline(v)\}
$$
### Spectral Radius
The **spectral radius** $rho(A)$ is the maximum modulus among all eigenvalues in the spectrum:
$$rho(A) = max_(lambda in sigma(A)) |lambda|$$
