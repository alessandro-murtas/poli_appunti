Let $A in CC^(n times n)$.
A pair $(lambda, v) in CC times (CC^n without {0})$ is called an **eigenpair** of $A$ if: 
$$
A v = lambda v
$$
* $lambda in CC$ is the **eigenvalue**.
* $bold(v) != bold(0)$ is the corresponding **eigenvector**.
## Rayleigh quotient
$forall i space.en (lambda_i, underline(v)_i)$ eigenpair of $A$:
$$
lambda_i=(underline(v)_i^H A underline(v)_i)/(underline(v)_i^H underline(v)_i)
$$
where $underline(v)^H=(underline(v)^T)^*$
