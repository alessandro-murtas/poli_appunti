# Eigenvalue problem
Given $A in CC^(n times n)$ find the [[Eigenvalues and eigenvectors|eigenpair]] $(lambda, v) in CC times {CC^n without {0}}$ s.t.
$$
A underline(v)=lambda underline(v)
$$
An eigenvalue is associated to infinite eigenvectors so for the problem to be [[Well posed problem|well posed]] we constrain $norm(v)=1$.
Methods:
- [[Power method]]
- [[Lanczos algorithm]]
