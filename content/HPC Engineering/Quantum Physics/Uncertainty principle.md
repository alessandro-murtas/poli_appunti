$$
\delta_{\hat{A}}^2\delta_{\hat{B}}^2\geq \left|\frac{1}{2i} \langle U\vert [\hat{A},\hat{B}] \vert U\rangle\right|^2
$$
The uncertainty principle defines a lower bound for the product of the standard deviation of 2 [[Observables|observables]].
If the [[Operators|operators]] do not [[Commutability|commute]] knowing exactly the value of one observables ($\delta_\hat{A}=0$) implies not knowing anything for the other ($\delta_\hat{A}=\infty$)
