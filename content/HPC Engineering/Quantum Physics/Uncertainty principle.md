The uncertainty principle defines a lower bound for the product of the standard deviation of 2 [[Observables|observables]].
If the [[Operators|operators]] do not [[Commutability|commute]] knowing exactly the value of one observables ($delta_hat(A)=0$) implies not knowing anything for the other ($delta_hat(A)= infinity$)
