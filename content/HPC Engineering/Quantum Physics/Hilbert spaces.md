A Hilbert space is a linear space with a scalar product.
Properties:
1. $ket(u),ket(v) in cal(H), alpha, beta in CC arrow.r.double.long alpha ket(u)+ beta ket(v) in cal(H)$
2. $ket(b_k) in cal(H)$ are called the basis vectors
    $$
    forall ket(u) in cal(H)space.quad  ket(u)=sum_(k=1)^n alpha_k ket(b_k)
   $$
    with $n$ being the *dimensionality* of the space.
3. Scalar product: $ket(u),ket(v) in cal(H) space.quad braket(u, v) in CC$
   Properties:
	1. $braket(u, alpha v)=alpha braket(u, v)$
	2. $braket(u,v)=braket(v,u)^*$
	3. $braket(u,v+omega)=braket(u,v)+braket(u,omega)$
	4. $braket(alpha u, beta v)=alpha^*beta braket(u,v)$
4. Norm: $abs(ket(u))= sqrt(braket(u)) in RR$
