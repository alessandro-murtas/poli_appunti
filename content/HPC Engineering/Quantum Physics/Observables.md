An observable is a quantity that can be measured in a system.
In a $ket("NH3")$ molecule an observable is the position of N atom (either above or below) and you can associate to each outcome a real number (0 and 1 for example).
#### Expectation value
The expectation value of an observable (the average outcome) is:
$$
expval(O)=abs(alpha)^2 dot 0 + abs(beta)^2 dot 1
$$
$$
expval(O^2)=abs(alpha)^2 dot (0)^2 + abs(beta)^2 dot (1)^2
$$
The standard deviation of an observable:
$$
sigma_O=sqrt(expval(O^2)-expval(O)^2)
$$
