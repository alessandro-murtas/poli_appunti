> [!definition]
> Let $A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$. A pair $(\lambda, \mathbf{v}) \in \mathbb{C} \times (\mathbb{C}^n \setminus \{\mathbf{0}\})$ is called an **eigenpair** of $A$ if: 
> $$A\mathbf{v} = \lambda\mathbf{v}$$
> * $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ is the **eigenvalue**.
> * $\mathbf{v} \neq \mathbf{0}$ is the corresponding **eigenvector**.
