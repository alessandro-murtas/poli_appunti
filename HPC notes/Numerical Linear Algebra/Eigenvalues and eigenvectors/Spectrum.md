Let $A \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$. The **spectrum** of $A$, denoted by $\sigma(A)$, is the set of all its [[Eigenvalues and eigenvectors|eigenvalues]]:
$$\sigma(A) = \{\lambda \in \mathbb{C} : \exists \underline{v} \in \mathbb{C}^n \setminus \{\underline{0}\} \text{ s.t. } A\underline{v} = \lambda\underline{v}\}$$
### Spectral Radius
The **spectral radius** $\rho(A)$ is the maximum modulus among all eigenvalues in the spectrum: $$\rho(A) = \max_{\lambda \in \sigma(A)} |\lambda|$$
